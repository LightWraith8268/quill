# Genre Overlay — Sci-Fi

Stacks on top of the base profile. Up to two overlays per story.

## Voice adjustments

- Tech vocabulary precise; no hand-wave verbs ("interfaced with", "accessed").
- Embed exposition inside action; never lecture mode.
- POV character treats their world as ordinary — no astonishment at routine tech.

## Worldbuilding rules

- Introduce one speculative element per scene; let readers acclimate before the next.
- Prefer concrete sensory detail of the future over abstraction (the smell of recycled air, not "the dystopian atmosphere").
- Names of factions, ships, AIs: chosen with internal consistency (linguistic family, naming conventions).

## Anti-patterns (genre-specific)

- "As you know, Bob" exposition between characters who already share knowledge.
- Tech magic — capability that solves problems on demand and is forgotten next chapter.
- Generic "the AI" / "the ship's computer" — name them or characterize them.
