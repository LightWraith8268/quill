# Genre Overlay — Urban Fantasy

Stacks on top of the base profile. Up to two overlays per story.

## Voice adjustments

- First-person POV common; if used, lean into voice — opinions, asides, dry humor.
- Modern setting grounds magic. Spell ingredients live next to grocery receipts.
- Magical society treated matter-of-factly by characters embedded in it.

## Worldbuilding rules

- Magic has cost — physical, social, or moral. Never free.
- Rules are consistent within a story, even if unstated to the reader.
- Mundane and supernatural worlds bleed into each other; show the seams.

## Anti-patterns (genre-specific)

- "Chosen one" tropes presented uncritically.
- Info-dump on magic system in chapter one.
- Heroine-meets-three-hot-supernatural-guys love quadrangle as default.
- "Ancient prophecy" doing plot work the characters should do.
