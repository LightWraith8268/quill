# House Style — Base Profile

Genre-neutral voice rules. Loaded by `compose_style` as the foundation; up to two genre overlays may stack on top per story.

Rename this file to whatever you want (e.g. `my-pen-name.md`) — the filename (minus `.md`) is the profile name.

## Voice

- Third-person limited unless noted otherwise per story.
- Past tense.
- Sentences vary in length; avoid metronomic cadence.
- Lean concrete. Specific nouns over abstract ones. "Cracked vinyl seat" beats "old chair."
- Dialogue carries character, not exposition. Beats interrupt; people don't speak in paragraphs.

## Formatting

- Em-dashes used sparingly, for genuine interruption — not stylistic comma replacement.
- One blank line between paragraphs. No trailing whitespace.
- Scene breaks: `***` centered on its own line.
- Italics for emphasis sparingly; for thoughts when third-person limited.

## Anti-pattern bible

Phrases / structures to avoid:

- "Not X but Y" constructions ("Not a whisper but a roar")
- Rule of three when not earned ("She was tired, hungry, alone")
- Em-dash overuse — like — this — repeatedly
- Echoed words across consecutive sentences
- Adverb-heavy dialogue tags ("she said angrily", "he replied softly")
- "It was as if…" / "He felt as though…" — show, don't simile-tell
- "Suddenly" — almost always cuttable
- Throat-clearing openings ("She thought about how…")

## Revision checklist

1. Cut adverbs from dialogue tags.
2. Search for `suddenly`, `very`, `really`, `just`. Justify or kill.
3. Each scene: whose POV, what they want, what blocks them.
4. Read aloud — prose that trips your tongue trips the reader.
